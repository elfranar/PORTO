Spearheading an end-to-end Carbon Capture and Utilization (CCU) research initiative under the supervision of Dr. Ir. Herman Hidayat, M.Si. Developing a continuous Dual-Bed reactor system utilizing alkali metal carbonate-based adsorbents and Cu/ZnO/Al2O3 catalysts to efficiently capture CO2 and convert it into methanol.

01 Process Overview

Fig. 1 — Diagram Alir Proses Sistem Reaktor (AutoCAD PFD Drawing)

  • MFC Mass Flow Control: Feed streams of CO₂ (44.01 g/hr), H₂ (6.05 g/hr), and N₂ (2.80 g/hr) are controlled using three Mass Flow Controllers (MFC) to maintain a precise stoichiometric H₂:CO₂ reactant ratio of 3:1 before mixing.
  • Gas Conditioning: The mixed gas enters the Guard Bed at 10 bar, experiencing a 0.5 bar pressure drop (outlet pressure: 9.5 bar, temperature: 23.3 °C) due to sulfur-filter packing resistance. It is then routed through a copper pre-heating coil, preheating it to the reactor inlet temperature of 250 °C (pre-heating duty: 8.35 W).
  • Reactor Zones: Reactants enter the tubular reactor containing three catalyst beds within a "Zona Panas" heated by split-shell electric heating mantles (energy duty: 11.94 W). Molar contraction from the reaction reduces the volumetric flow from 18.80 L/hr to 11.98 L/hr. The hot product gas is then cooled to 50 °C in a "Zona Dingin" pipe cooling coil (energy duty: 21.62 W).
  • Separation & Recovery: Gaseous methanol and water are condensed in a Cold Trap/Condenser at 50 °C. Shortcut distillation separation yields pure methanol (22.14 g/hr) and wastewater (12.96 g/hr).
  • Pressure Control: Operating pressure is maintained at 9.5 bar using a simple Back Pressure Regulator (BPR) manual needle valve.
  • Dry Gas Analysis: Uncondensed dry gases (Gas Sisa: 17.75 g/hr) flow through a Gas Drying Unit, a Wet Test Meter (WTM) to measure flow volume, and a GC Injection Port for gas chromatography analysis before venting.

Design Selection Rationale: The continuous multi-bed fixed-bed design divides the catalyst mass into three distinct zones (Bed 1, 2, and 3) separated by quartz wool, allowing temperature profiles to be controlled. The choice of Swagelok compression fittings and tube reducers (10 mm to 6 mm) ensures leak-proof micro-scale operations at the 9.5 bar reaction pressure while maintaining the safety margin of a 75 bar mechanical design rating. Split-shell electric heating mantles (300 °C) provide precise heat transfer to catalyst beds, monitored by dual thermocouples (T1 & T2).
04 EFB Leaching Kinetics Modeling

Kinetics Modeling: Analyzed the leaching kinetics of 11 essential metal ions from oil palm Empty Fruit Bunch (EFB) ash, which serves as a bio-derived alkali catalyst source. Modeled experimental concentration profiles over time against first-order (Lagergren) and pseudo-second-order (Ho & McKay) rate equations to calculate saturation capacity (Csat) and rate coefficients (k1, k2).

  • Pseudo-Second-Order (Ho & McKay): Assumes that the rate-limiting step is chemical sorption/dissolution involving valence forces through sharing or exchange of electrons between the EFB ash matrix and the solvent.

05 Material & Safety Consideration
Material Selection
  • SS316L Tubing & Fittings: Selected for reactor tubes and process lines because it provides exceptional mechanical strength under pressure and superior resistance to hydrogen embrittlement and carbonic acid corrosion from wet CO₂.
  • Quartz Wool Bed Dividers: Installed between catalyst beds to act as high-temperature gas-permeable barriers, securing the catalyst pellets in place and preventing fluidization or channel blockages.
  • Heating & Cooling Zoning: Designed with separate heating mantles for the 250 °C catalytic reaction zone and a cooling coil for product condensation, ensuring precise temperature profiles.
Leak Prevention & Safety
  • Double-Ferrule Swagelok Unions: Ensures absolute leak prevention for hazardous H₂ gas under 9.5 bar operating conditions.
  • Thermocouple Wells: Integrated dual probe ports allow direct, localized monitoring of the catalyst bed temperature profile to prevent thermal runaway or hot-spots.
  • Safety Valve Integration: Upstream overpressure relief system and downstream check valves prevent accidental backflow or explosive venting.

08 Reflection & Improvement
What I Learned

Gained comprehensive experience designing an industrial-scale reactor system end-to-end. I learned how to translate theoretical reaction kinetics and mass balances into mechanical AutoCAD equipment designs, developing a deep understanding of thermal management limitations in highly exothermic catalytic operations.

Limitations

The project's design and operating parameters are strictly modeled under ideal, steady-state simulations using literature kinetic rate constants. Real-world catalyst decay, localized hot-spots, and flow distribution abnormalities inside the tubes can only be verified with physical laboratory scale validation.

Next Steps

My next step is to further study and simulate the transient process flows in DWSIM to analyze startup behavior and feed variations. Furthermore, I aim to conduct experimental validation using micro-reactor tests once laboratory-scale testing begins.
