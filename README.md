# Elfrana De Ridwan — Personal Portfolio

Welcome to my personal portfolio.

I am a Chemistry undergraduate at Universitas Indonesia with a strong interest in understanding how scientific knowledge can be applied beyond the laboratory. Although my academic background is rooted in Chemistry, my curiosity has gradually led me to explore areas such as engineering, data analysis, programming, and computational work.

Throughout my studies, I have worked on various academic and research projects, including laboratory analysis, computational chemistry, kinetic modeling, and process engineering. My experience at the National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN) further introduced me to reactor design, process simulation, and data-driven analysis in the context of sustainable energy research.

At the same time, I began learning programming independently. What started as simple curiosity developed into an interest in building software, working with data, and understanding how technology can be used to solve practical problems. Many of the projects presented here were built as part of that self-directed learning process.

This portfolio brings those experiences together — from Chemistry and research to engineering, data, and software development.

## What You Will Find Here

- **Research & Engineering** — Academic and research work related to Chemistry, process engineering, reactor design, and sustainable energy.
- **Data & Computational Work** — Quantitative analysis, kinetic modeling, simulation, and data interpretation.
- **Software & Programming** — Self-directed projects built while learning programming and web development.
- **Experience** — Selected academic, research, laboratory, and organizational experiences.

## Why This Portfolio Exists

I do not see my Chemistry background as a limitation to exploring other fields. Instead, it has given me a foundation in analytical thinking, problem solving, and scientific reasoning that I can apply while learning new disciplines.

This portfolio represents that process of exploration and continuous learning.

I am still learning, experimenting, and building — and this portfolio will continue to grow along with those experiences.
